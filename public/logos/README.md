# Company logo assets

39 real company logos: 24 SVG and 15 PNG. No placeholders or omitted symbols.

Use `manifest.json` as the symbol-to-filename mapping. Every entry records its source page, asset URL, brand color, color provenance, known license information and SHA-256 checksum. Files are self-contained and work on a white background.

Sources: 20 Simple Icons assets (18 pinned to commit d4e6ba93e48f178898707f0145ec285f28b64b38; Amazon and Oracle from v11.15.0), 3 SVGL assets, 15 CompaniesMarketCap PNG assets and 1 Wikimedia Commons LVMH SVG.

Simple Icons geometry is unchanged; only the root fill uses its published brand color. Other delivered files are unchanged downloads. Some companies use full wordmarks because the verified source supplied that identity. Google represents GOOGL. The shared Volvo brand mark represents VOLV-B.ST.

Simple Icons collection: CC0-1.0. SVGL collection: MIT. Company logo trademark rights remain with the respective owners. CompaniesMarketCap does not state an asset-specific reuse license on those company pages. Sampled PNG colors are image-derived values, not official brand specifications.

LVMH attribution: LVMH; vector by Moonrivers, Wikimedia Commons, 2023-09-06. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:LVMH_wordmark.svg . Uploader license: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . The source page also labels the underlying text logo PD-textlogo. No modifications.
